2. METHOD DESCRIPTION
----------------------------------------
  The deduplication pipeline uses three sequential passes:
    Pass 1 - DOI exact match: papers sharing the same normalised DOI
            are grouped; the most complete record is kept.
    Pass 2 - Exact normalised title: among remaining papers, those with
            identical normalised titles (NFKD + lowercase + '&'->'and'
            + strip punctuation) are grouped; the most complete is kept.
    Pass 3 - TF-IDF cosine similarity: among remaining papers, titles
            are vectorised with character n-grams (3-4); pairs with
            cosine similarity >= 0.85 are merged, keeping the more
            complete record.

  Evaluation uses GROUP-AWARE metrics: a ground-truth duplicate is
  counted as detected (TP) if any member of its duplicate group was
  flagged for removal. This eliminates wrong-survivor bias where the
  algorithm correctly identifies a duplicate pair but removes a
  different member than the one labelled 'DUPLICATE' in the ground truth.
